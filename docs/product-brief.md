
# Product Brief

A one-page personal roadmap board for planning high-level milestones and work blocks across 2026.

The app is designed around cognitive bandwidth, not task tracking.

Primary use case:

“I want to see the major commitments and intentions occupying my year, grouped by workstream, without managing weekly tasks.”

Core lanes:
- TreePlet Core
- Learning
- Personal Strategic
- Optional / Sandbox

The app should help the user answer:
- What am I focused on this quarter?
- What major milestones are coming?
- Where is the year overloaded?
- Which items are commitments versus intentions?
